Activities & Outdoor Experiences

Choose your style of travel: adventure seeking, historical discovery, or culinary tasting.

Outdoor

Adventure & Nature

• Spelunking and river kayaking in the limestone caverns of Basey, Samar.

• Waterfall chasing across the volcanic island province of Biliran.

• Beach camping and snorkeling along Kalanggaman and Sambawan sandbars.

Heritage

Culture & History

• Walk the historic Red Beach MacArthur Landing Memorial Park in Palo.

• Visit Spanish colonial stone watchtowers on Capul Island.

• Attend hands-on mat weaving workshops with Samar artisans.

Culinary

Food & Dining

• Taste freshly cooked Binagol and Chocolate Moron in Tacloban City.

• Experience floating restaurant dining along Sohoton River.

• Enjoy fresh seafood grills in coastal Palompon, Leyte.
