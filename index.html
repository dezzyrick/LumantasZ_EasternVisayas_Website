About Our Region

Eastern Visayas (Region VIII) comprises three main islands—Leyte, Samar, and Biliran—surrounded by turquoise waters and rich natural landscapes. Known for its historical landmarks such as the MacArthur Leyte Landing Memorial, the iconic San Juanico Bridge, and majestic cave systems, Eastern Visayas offers an extraordinary mix of adventure, tradition, and warm hospitality.

Kalanggaman Island

World-famous for its long, crystal-clear white sandbar extending gracefully into turquoise waters.
