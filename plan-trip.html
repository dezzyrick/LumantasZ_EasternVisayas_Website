Travel Essentials & Tips

Pro Tip

Smart Advice

• Book Kalanggaman Island boat slots early with the Palompon Tourism Office.

• Carry Philippine Peso cash (₱); ATMs are sparse in small island municipalities.

• Respect marine sanctuaries and leave no trace during river/cave explorations.

Checklist

Packing Checklist

• Waterproof dry bags for phone, camera, and travel documents.

• Sturdy aqua shoes or water sandals for walking inside limestone caves.

• Coral-friendly sunscreen, brimmed hat, and eco-friendly insect repellent.
